Tours

Travel with us and let us guide you to the most beautiful places of Paros. Discover Naoussa with its refined atmosphere and elegant whitewashed alleys. Visit Lefkes, a traditional village known for its authentic cafés and timeless charm. Walk through Parikia and explore the historic Panagia Ekatontapyliani, one of the oldest churches in Greece, surrounded by charming narrow streets. Indulge in exclusive wine tasting at local wineries and enjoy a truly memorable experience, a horseback riding at sunrise or sunset, capturing the magic of the island.

  • Private guided tours
  • Local winery visits
  • Horseriding at sunrise or sunset
